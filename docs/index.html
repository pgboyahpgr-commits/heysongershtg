About HeySongers

HeySongers is a free, open-source music streaming app built with Flutter by Ruthvik Pedapondara (geethudinoyt). It lets you stream any song from YouTube Music and YouTube, download tracks for offline playback, and enjoy a beautiful, smooth experience — all without paying a single penny.

We believe music should be accessible to everyone. That's why HeySongers is and always will be 100% free — no subscription fees, no hidden costs, and zero ads.

Features

Stream Any Song

Access millions of songs from YouTube Music and YouTube instantly.

Offline Playback

Download your favorite tracks and listen anywhere, anytime — no internet needed.

Zero Ads

No interruptions. No banners. No video ads. Just pure, uninterrupted music.

Privacy First

No tracking, no data collection, no selling your information. Your listening stays yours.

High Quality Audio

Enjoy your music in the best available quality.

Playlist Management

Create, edit, and organize your playlists effortlessly.

Background Playback

Listen while using other apps or with your screen off.

Dark & Light Themes

Choose the look that suits your mood.

Why HeySongers?

There are plenty of music streaming services out there, but most come with a catch — monthly fees, relentless ads, or invasive tracking. HeySongers is different:

  • 100% Free — No subscriptions, no premium tiers
  • No Ads — Not a single advertisement, ever
  • No Tracking — Your data stays on your device
  • Open Source — The code is public. Audit it, modify it, trust it
  • Offline First — Download and listen without internet
